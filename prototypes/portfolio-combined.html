Why QLoRA + Unsloth

Full fine-tuning a 7B model needs 80 GB+ VRAM — not on a free T4 (16 GB). QLoRA fine-tunes low-rank adapter matrices only, cutting memory 4×. Unsloth adds 2× speed via kernel optimisations. Together they make the free Kaggle tier genuinely viable for real training.
